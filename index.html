about

practice

studio 1/1 is a collective of designers and makers. Our mission is to design spaces and create objects that carry a deep meaning for their users while holding high regard toward the beauty and the poetic value of the existing environment.

With an open mind, we explore different possibilities. We search for a solution that fits within your story, all while authenticity, sustainability, and a strong technical logic remain at the foundation of our practice. We strive to create high-quality projects with a personal value.

service

We offer you a tailor-made project that fully responds to your needs and interests. Through in-depth research, we strive to bring together functionality, materiality and construction techniques in a well-considered architectural design.

For this reason, we place great importance on clear communication, both with you as our client and with our construction partners. We closely follow the project down to the smallest details and maintain a thorough understanding of the entire process, from design through to completion, in order to ensure the highest quality.

studio 1/1 supports you throughout the entire process of bringing your project to life, from furniture design to architecture. Beyond design, we take care of all technical and administrative matters.
You decide, we guide.
